About Chamyaia Collective

Founded by designer Shagun Sharma, Chamyaia Collective represents a fusion of traditional craftsmanship and contemporary design. Each piece tells a story, crafted with meticulous attention to detail and a passion for unique self-expression.

We believe jewelry should be as individual as the person wearing it. Every creation is a wearable work of art, designed to empower and inspire.
